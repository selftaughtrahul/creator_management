To remove a connected Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or X account, sign in to Awisee, open Connections, and choose Disconnect. That deletes the saved token and the posts and metrics stored for that account.

To remove the Awisee account itself, sign in and delete the user from the Awisee admin, or email the address listed on the Meta app’s contact email and ask for the account to be deleted. Deletion removes the profile, connected-network records, and stored analytics for that user.
